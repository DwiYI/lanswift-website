Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LanSwift ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile and desktop application.

1. Local-Only File Transfers & Discovery

LanSwift is designed to transfer files and folders directly between your devices on your local Wi-Fi or local area network (LAN).

  • No Cloud Storage: Files you send or receive are never uploaded to our servers or third-party file servers.
  • Local Network Discovery: To discover nearby devices running LanSwift on the same Wi-Fi network, the App broadcasts and responds to UDP beacons containing your configured device name, platform type, a randomly generated local device ID, and transfer port number.
  • Web Portal Mode: When you enable the HTTP Web App Server, files in your Send Queue are served locally over your LAN IP address protected by a randomly generated 4-digit PIN session code for authorized devices you share the URL/QR code with.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores preferences locally on your device using standard platform storage (such as Android SharedPreferences):

  • Custom device name and locally generated random device ID
  • Transfer preferences (download folder path, auto-accept setting, folder organization, keep-awake preference, and background radar toggle)
  • Local Send Queue / Web Portal file path list and in-memory diagnostic logs (which you can clear at any time in Settings)

3. Advertising & User Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

On mobile platforms, LanSwift displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. While LanSwift itself does not operate user accounts or analytics servers, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may automatically collect and process certain data to serve ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, and manage regulatory consent (such as GDPR in the EEA/UK and applicable privacy laws), including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID / IDFA) and app set IDs
  • IP address (used to estimate general/approximate location and network connection)
  • Device information, ad impressions, interactions, and crash/performance diagnostics
  • User consent choices and preferences recorded via the Google UMP consent framework

Managing Your Ad Preferences: If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you can review, update, or revoke your advertising consent choices at any time directly within the App by navigating to Settings → Legal, Privacy & About → Ad Privacy Choices. You can also manage personalized ad preferences in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at Google Partner Sites Policy.

4. App Permissions

  • Wi-Fi / Local Network Access: Required to discover peers and transfer files over your local Wi-Fi or LAN, as well as to fetch AdMob ads when internet is available.
  • File & Storage Access: Used strictly to read files/folders you explicitly choose to send. On modern Android versions, received files are saved into your public Downloads folder via Android's scoped MediaStore API without requiring broad device storage permissions.
  • Notifications: Used on Android 13+ to display transfer alerts and completion notifications.
  • Foreground Service (Data Sync): Used to keep active transfers running reliably and optionally maintain local device discovery when the app is minimized. The ongoing notification includes a direct "Stop Radar" action button, and background discovery can be enabled or disabled at any time under Settings → Transfer Behavior.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption (Optional): You may optionally grant unrestricted background battery usage so your phone does not throttle incoming transfers while idle.

5. Children's Privacy

LanSwift is a general-audience utility application and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
