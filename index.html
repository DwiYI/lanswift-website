FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does LanSwift need internet?

No. Transfers happen directly between devices on your local network. Internet is only used by the optional ads in the app.

Is there a file-size limit?

No. Files are streamed in chunks, so very large videos and folders work fine as long as the receiver has storage.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Never. Files go device-to-device. We don't operate servers that see your content.

My transfer stopped when the phone slept — now what?

LanSwift keeps the screen on during active transfers and resumes automatically from the last confirmed byte. For best results, allow the battery-optimization exemption when prompted.

Can someone without the app receive files?

Yes — share the web-portal link or QR code and they can use any modern browser on the same network.

Is LanSwift free?

Yes, it's free and supported by unobtrusive ads.
